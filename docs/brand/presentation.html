Final design

Overline Sheet

A dog-eared lead sheet whose interior cutouts form chord badges perched directly above lyric bars.

  • Encodes the app's signature overline-chord typographic layout directly into the negative space of a song sheet.
  • 45° top-right fold with a 16-unit orthogonal channel creates an unmistakable document silhouette at every scale.
  • Includes a dedicated 1-stanza small-size cut for 16×16 favicons and an irradiation-compensated reversed cut for dark stage mode.
